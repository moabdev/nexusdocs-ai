## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the rules governing shipment acceptance, service levels, tracking, delivery, delays, loss classification, claims, and standard compensation for Velox Logistics services.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to domestic shipments handled through Velox Logistics distribution centers and last-mile delivery operations. Contract-specific service-level agreements may override this policy only when explicitly documented.

## 3. Definitions

- **Business day:** Monday through Friday, excluding applicable public holidays.
- **Claim:** Formal customer request for investigation and possible compensation.
- **Lost shipment:** Shipment that meets the loss threshold and cannot be located through operational tracing.
- **Delivery exception:** Event preventing delivery within the expected service window.
- **Standard service:** Regular delivery service without expedited handling.
- **Express service:** Priority delivery service with expedited handling.

## 4. Service Levels

| Service | Target delivery window |
|---|---|
| Standard | 5-8 business days |
| Express | 2-3 business days |

Delivery windows begin on the first business day after shipment acceptance. Remote areas, force majeure, customs procedures, address corrections, and recipient-requested changes may extend these targets.

## 5. Shipment Acceptance

Shipments must contain a valid sender and recipient address, contact information, declared contents when required, and packaging appropriate to the shipment's weight and fragility. Velox Logistics may refuse prohibited, unsafe, incorrectly packaged, or materially misdeclared shipments.

## 6. Packaging Requirements

The sender is responsible for packaging goods to withstand normal sorting, handling, transportation, and last-mile delivery. Fragile goods must use internal cushioning and an external container appropriate to the contents. Damage attributable solely to inadequate packaging may be excluded from standard compensation.

## 7. Tracking

Each accepted shipment receives a unique tracking identifier. Operational events may include PICKED_UP, IN_TRANSIT, AT_DISTRIBUTION_CENTER, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERED, DELIVERY_EXCEPTION, and RETURN_TO_SENDER. Tracking events represent the latest recorded operational state and may be corrected after investigation.

## 8. Delivery Procedures

Delivery may be completed to the named recipient or another authorized person at the destination when permitted by the service. Proof of delivery may include timestamp, recipient confirmation, delivery scan, or other evidence supported by the operating region.

## 9. Delayed Shipments

A shipment is delayed when it exceeds its applicable target delivery window without a documented exception that suspends the service target. Customers should consult tracking information before opening a formal claim.

## 10. Lost Shipments

A shipment may be classified as potentially lost after **10 business days** without a valid tracking progression or confirmed delivery, subject to operational investigation. Classification as potentially lost does not itself guarantee compensation.

## 11. Claims

Claims relating to loss, non-delivery, or eligible shipment damage must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the relevant delivery event, expected delivery date, or qualifying incident, as applicable.

The claimant must provide the tracking identifier and sufficient information to support investigation. Additional evidence may be required under the Claims and Refund Policy.

## 12. Compensation

For eligible claims under the standard compensation framework, the maximum compensation is **R$ 1,000** per shipment, subject to documented value, exclusions, contractual terms, and the Claims and Refund Policy.

The compensation ceiling is not an automatic payment amount.

## 13. Exceptions

Velox Logistics may exclude or limit liability when delay, loss, or damage results from prohibited contents, inadequate packaging, incorrect recipient information, fraud, force majeure, government action, or other exclusions established by contract or applicable policy.

## 14. Responsibilities

The Operations Department owns this policy and maintains shipping procedures. Customer Experience coordinates customer-facing claims. Technology maintains systems supporting tracking and operational records. Employees must apply the policy version effective for the relevant shipment or historical period.
