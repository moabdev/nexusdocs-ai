## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how Velox Logistics receives, validates, investigates, resolves, and compensates eligible customer claims involving shipment loss, non-delivery, damage, and service failures.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to eligible domestic shipments handled by Velox Logistics. It complements the current Shipping Policy and does not replace operational delivery windows, loss thresholds, or compensation ceilings established there.

## 3. Governing Policy Relationship

The ACTIVE Shipping Policy determines current service windows, the threshold at which a shipment may be treated as potentially lost, the deadline for filing eligible shipping claims, and the maximum standard compensation ceiling.

Claims personnel must consult the ACTIVE version applicable to the shipment. Historical or SUPERSEDED shipping policies may be used only when evaluating shipments governed by those historical periods.

## 4. Claim Categories

Velox Logistics recognizes the following principal claim categories:

- **LOSS:** shipment cannot be located after the applicable operational loss threshold.
- **NON_DELIVERY:** tracking or delivery evidence conflicts with the customer's report of receipt.
- **DAMAGE:** contents or packaging show eligible transportation-related damage.
- **SERVICE_FAILURE:** an eligible contracted service commitment was not met.
- **BILLING_ADJUSTMENT:** an incorrect eligible transportation charge requires correction.

## 5. Eligibility

A claim is eligible for review when it is submitted within the applicable deadline, concerns a covered shipment, contains the required evidence, and is not excluded by contract or policy.

Opening a claim does not guarantee reimbursement or compensation.

## 6. Required Evidence

Every claim must include:

1. shipment tracking identifier;
2. claimant name and contact information;
3. description of the incident;
4. date the issue was discovered;
5. requested resolution.

Loss or non-delivery claims may additionally require proof of item value or purchase.

Damage claims must include photographs of the external packaging, internal packaging, and damaged contents when reasonably available.

Velox Logistics may request invoices, receipts, commercial documents, recipient declarations, proof of delivery records, or other evidence required for investigation.

## 7. Delivered but Not Received

When tracking shows `DELIVERED` but the recipient reports that the shipment was not received, Customer Experience must not immediately classify the shipment as lost.

The case must follow the waiting and verification procedure defined in the Tracking Operations Manual before a formal non-delivery investigation is opened.

After the required waiting period, the team must verify available delivery evidence and, when necessary, escalate the case to Operations.

## 8. Lost Shipment Claims

A shipment becomes eligible for loss investigation only after reaching the operational loss threshold defined by the ACTIVE Shipping Policy.

Customer Experience validates the claim documentation and creates the case. Operations performs the operational trace and determines whether the shipment can be located.

A potentially lost classification is not itself a final compensation decision.

## 9. Damage Claims

Customers should preserve the shipment, contents, and packaging until the investigation is completed or Velox Logistics confirms that retention is no longer required.

Damage caused exclusively by inadequate packaging, prohibited contents, inherent product defects, or exclusions established by contract may be denied.

## 10. Investigation Workflow

The standard workflow is:

1. Customer Experience receives the claim.
2. Required evidence is validated.
3. The case is categorized and assigned a case identifier.
4. Operational evidence is collected.
5. Operations performs tracing when required.
6. Fraud or material inconsistency indicators are escalated.
7. Eligibility and compensation are evaluated.
8. The customer receives the resolution.

## 11. Investigation Service Levels

Initial claim validation should occur within **2 business days** after submission.

Standard investigations should normally be completed within **5 business days** after all required evidence is available.

Complex cases requiring cross-region tracing, fraud review, or third-party evidence may require up to **10 business days**.

These investigation targets do not modify the filing deadline established by the governing Shipping Policy.

## 12. Compensation

Approved compensation is based on documented eligible loss and remains subject to the maximum standard compensation ceiling established by the ACTIVE Shipping Policy.

The ceiling is not an automatic payment amount. If documented eligible value is lower than the applicable ceiling, compensation is limited to the documented eligible value.

Contract-specific insurance or declared-value arrangements may establish different limits.

## 13. Refunds and Billing Adjustments

Eligible refunds may be issued for qualifying billing errors or covered service failures. Shipping-charge refunds are evaluated independently from compensation for lost or damaged goods unless the applicable contract states otherwise.

Approved refunds must be returned through an authorized payment or account-credit process.

## 14. Fraud and Abuse Controls

Claims containing materially inconsistent information, manipulated evidence, repeated suspicious patterns, or identity concerns must be escalated for fraud review.

Employees must not accuse a customer of fraud solely because a claim was selected for additional verification.

## 15. Escalation

Customer Experience owns customer communication and claim administration.

Operations owns shipment tracing and operational evidence.

Finance validates authorized payments when required.

Cases involving suspected fraud, significant legal exposure, security incidents, or exceptions beyond delegated authority must be escalated to the appropriate responsible function.

## 16. Denied Claims

A denied claim must include a documented reason. Common reasons include:

- filing outside the applicable deadline;
- insufficient evidence after reasonable opportunity to provide it;
- excluded or prohibited contents;
- inadequate packaging when directly responsible for damage;
- unsupported claimed value;
- contractually excluded event;
- confirmed successful delivery with sufficient evidence.

## 17. Records and Auditability

Claim records must preserve the case identifier, shipment identifier, evidence references, investigation actions, decision, responsible function, timestamps, and any authorized compensation or refund.

The record must be sufficient to reconstruct why the decision was made.

## 18. Policy Precedence

For current shipments, this policy must be interpreted together with the ACTIVE Shipping Policy and applicable operational manuals.

If a conflict exists regarding shipping service windows, loss thresholds, filing deadlines, or standard compensation ceilings, the ACTIVE Shipping Policy controls unless a more specific authorized contract applies.
